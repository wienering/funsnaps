Where we are

160 Applewood Crescent, Concord, ON L4K 4E5. That is Vaughan, just off Highway 7, and it is a working shop rather than a showroom, so come by arrangement rather than unannounced.

We cover the whole GTA from there with no travel fees: Vaughan, Toronto, Mississauga, Oakville, Richmond Hill, Markham and the York Region towns to the north.
