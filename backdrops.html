Choosing a backdrop for your room

A backdrop does two jobs. It gives every photo a clean, consistent frame, and it tells guests across the room where the booth is. The best choice is the one that suits the venue rather than fights it.

Match the room, not just the theme

A banquet hall with gold and crystal already has a palette. A backdrop that sits inside it looks intentional in the prints; one that clashes looks like it arrived in a van. If you are unsure, send us a photo of the room and we will tell you what we would pick.

Mind the ceiling

Most setups want about ten feet of height. Some backdrops work in less, which matters for basements, tents and older halls. Let us know the venue and we will show you the options that fit.

Pair it with the right booth

Bright, textured backdrops suit the open-air booth and a busy dance floor. If you are booking the glam booth, a simpler backdrop keeps the black-and-white portrait look clean. For brand events, the corporate setup can put your own artwork behind guests instead.

Common questions

Is a backdrop included with a photo booth package?

Yes. Every package includes one backdrop, set up by the attendant before your booked start.

How much space does a backdrop need?

Roughly ten feet wide by eight deep for the booth, backdrop and prop table together. Some options work under lower ceilings, so tell us the venue if the room is tight.

Can we use our own branded backdrop?

Yes. For corporate events we key your step-and-repeat artwork in behind guests with a green screen, so nothing has to be shipped into the room.
