Formats, all the same price

The booth format is a decision about your room, not your budget. We price all of them identically so you are free to pick on fit.

  • Open-air booth. The default. About a dozen people in one frame and a visible queue that recruits the next group.
  • Classic enclosed booth. Curtain, tight retro strip, a bit of privacy. Suits a smaller room.
  • Glam booth. High-key black and white, the press-wall look. Previously a $75 add-on, now included at no charge.
  • 360 video booth. Orbiting arm, slow-motion clips sent to phones. Needs about ten feet of ceiling clearance.

Add-ons

Only two things are charged on top of the package, and both are listed here rather than buried.

  • Unlimited prints — $60 per hour. The standard package prints two copies per round, which is enough for a guest to keep one and put one in the book. Unlimited prints gives every person in the frame their own copy. Worth it for large group-heavy events, unnecessary for most weddings.
  • Additional waiting time — $50 per hour. Only applies where you want us on site with the booth closed for a long gap, for example a very long break between a ceremony and a reception. Normal setup and teardown are never charged.

Deposit and payment

A $100 deposit secures your date and comes off your final balance, which is due by or on the event day. There are no travel fees anywhere we go, from downtown Toronto to Newmarket, and we do not bill parking back to you either.

What corporate bookings include

Corporate jobs get the custom logo template at no extra charge, and the virtual green screen so your own step-and-repeat can sit behind the guest. We need vector artwork at least a week ahead. Certificates of insurance naming your venue as additional insured are available on request. Full detail on the corporate page.

Working out which package you need

More hours is not automatically better. A booth has two peaks, cocktails and the late dance floor, with a long quiet stretch through dinner and speeches in between. Booking straight through that middle stretch buys you an idle booth.

If you send your running order with your enquiry we will tell you where the hours actually belong, which fairly often means a smaller package used well rather than a bigger one used badly. The hour-by-hour breakdown on the Markham page walks through a full night if you want to see how it plays out.
