Four ways to run it.

Open-air booth

The default, and what most nights want. About a dozen people fit in one frame and the queue is visible to the room, which is what pulls the next group in.

Classic enclosed

Curtain, tight retro strip, a bit of privacy. Suits a smaller room, or a crowd that wants the old-fashioned booth rather than the party version.

Glam booth

High-key lighting, skin smoothing, black-and-white output. The look people already associate with a press wall, and the one guests post without being asked.

360 video

A platform with an arm that orbits the guest. Reads as an attraction even when nobody's using it, which is why it works at trade shows and launches.
